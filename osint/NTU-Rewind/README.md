# NTU Rewind

### Author: Mujabeast

Deep in an NTU research lab, genius student Sann Tynell finally completed his homemade time machine. Before he could celebrate, the device slipped from his hands and activated.

He stumbled forward and nearly tripped over something. When he turned around, he froze.

A cat. Not just any cat. The cutest cat he had ever seen.

And it was READING.

His time machine buzzed again. Somehow, it had connected to the free NTU Wi-Fi. He checked the date: April 2025.

What did the cat read?
