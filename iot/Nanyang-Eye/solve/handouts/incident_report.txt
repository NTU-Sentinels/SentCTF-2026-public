  NTU SOC — INCIDENT REPORT (DECLASSIFIED FOR EXERCISE)
  Ref: NANYANG-EYE-03 / PROJECT ORION
================================================================================

SOC recovered a busy maintenance capture from the network path used by a
retired D-Link pan/tilt camera. The original device is no longer directly
reachable. A restricted CCTV Operations Console has been provided for the
final physical operation.

Your objective is to separate ordinary network and video traffic from the
legacy camera-management exchange, recover the maintenance protocol, and
repair the supplied Python client. The capture contains routine camera,
recorder and LAN activity; use protocol and request filters rather than manual
packet browsing.

Complete and dry-run the client on your own laptop before attending the
physical Operations Console. The console starts at a normal terminal prompt
with a fresh working copy of the client; no editor opens automatically. Use
normal terminal tools if you need to reproduce your completed local work.

The device does not use ONVIF in this scenario. Treat the observed legacy CGI
form as the authoritative PTZ protocol. The physical D-Link administration
network is out of scope; the supplied client reaches only an isolated gateway.
