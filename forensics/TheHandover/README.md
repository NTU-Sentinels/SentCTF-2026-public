# The Handover

### Author: Rushaidy

NTU's Cyber Security Office intercepted an encrypted forensic image recovered from the laptop of a researcher suspected of leaking confidential project data. Hidden within are several encrypted and encoded files containing the details of an upcoming rendezvous.

Your task is to analyze the evidence and uncover the meeting location, date, and time before the data exchange takes place.

Flag format: `sentctf{...}` <br>
Note: ` The flag is the meeting location, date, and time in the format: sentctf{location_date_time} `
